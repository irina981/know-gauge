# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

Corvane supports flexible working because it helps people do their best work and balance work with their personal
lives. We also believe that time together in person builds trust, speeds up learning and keeps our culture strong.
This policy explains the working arrangements we offer and the rules that keep flexible work fair, safe and secure.

## 2. Working arrangements

| Arrangement | Description | Who approves |
|---|---|---|
| Office-based | Works from a Corvane office at least 4 days per week. Typical for reception, facilities and lab roles. | Manager |
| Hybrid (default) | Works from the office at least **2 days per week**, one of which is the team's **anchor day**. | Manager |
| Remote | Works from home most of the time and visits the office at least once per quarter. Must live in a country where a Corvane entity employs people. | Manager and the VP of the department, recorded in the employment contract |

**Anchor days** are agreed by each team and published on the team's intranet page. Teams should hold planning
sessions, retrospectives and social activities on anchor days. New joiners are expected to come to the office at
least **3 days per week during their first 4 weeks**, to get to know their team.

## 3. Working hours and availability

- **Core collaboration hours** are **10:00 to 15:00** local time, Monday to Friday. During core hours you should be
  available for meetings and messages, except when you are on leave or have agreed otherwise with your manager.
- Outside core hours, you can organise your own working time, within your contracted hours and local working-time
  law.
- Keep your Teams status and calendar up to date, so colleagues know when you are available.
- **Right to disconnect**: you are not expected to read or reply to messages outside your working hours, unless you are
  on an agreed on-call rota. Managers should use "schedule send" for messages written outside normal hours.

## 4. Working from another location

### Within your own country

You can work from another location in your country of employment (for example a holiday home), as long as you meet
the security rules in section 6 and can attend anchor days.

### Working abroad temporarily

Working from another country can create tax, social security and immigration obligations for you and for Corvane.
Therefore:

- You may work from another **EU/EEA country or Switzerland** for up to **20 working days per calendar year**.
- You must request it in PeoplePortal at least **10 working days in advance**. Both your manager and the People Team
  must approve it.
- Working from a country **outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland** needs additional approval from the Legal and Tax teams,
  and the request must be submitted at least **30 days in advance**. Approval is not guaranteed.
- Working from a country under EU sanctions is **never** allowed, even for a day.
- Customer data must not be accessed from outside the EEA unless the customer contract allows it. Check with the DPO.

## 5. Allowances and equipment

- Hybrid and remote employees receive a monthly **internet allowance of €30**, paid with the salary.
- A one-off **home workstation allowance of up to €400** is available (see the Ergonomics and Home Workstation Guide).
- Corvane covers travel costs to the office for **remote** employees when they are asked to attend an event or
  meeting. Commuting costs for hybrid employees follow the local commuting allowance rules.

## 6. Security when working outside the office

- Use only your Corvane laptop for work. Connect to the **Corvane VPN** on any network that is not your home network.
- Make sure nobody else, including family members, can see your screen or use your device. Lock your screen every
  time you step away.
- Do not hold calls about Confidential or Restricted topics in public places such as trains, cafés or co-working
  spaces, where others can overhear you. Use a privacy screen filter in public.
- Do not print Confidential or Restricted documents at home.
- Your home Wi-Fi must be protected with a strong password and WPA2 or WPA3 encryption. Change the default router
  admin password.

## 7. Changing your arrangement

You can request a change of working arrangement at any time through PeoplePortal. Your manager must respond in writing
within **4 weeks**. A request can only be refused for a business reason, which must be explained. If you disagree, you
can ask your People Partner to review the decision.

Corvane may ask hybrid employees to come to the office more often for a limited period, for example during a major
product launch, with at least **2 weeks' notice**.

## 8. Health and wellbeing

Remote and hybrid work can blur the line between work and private life. Plan breaks, end your day with a clear
"shutdown" routine and stay in touch with your team. The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers free, confidential
support 24/7 (see the Leave and Time Off Policy).
