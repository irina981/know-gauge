# Leave and Time Off Policy

## 1. Purpose

Rest and time away from work are essential for health, wellbeing and good work. This policy describes the leave
Corvane offers. Entitlements are shown for a full-time employee (40 hours per week) and are calculated **pro rata**
for part-time employees. All leave is requested and recorded in **PeoplePortal**.

## 2. Annual leave

- Full-time employees receive **25 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays.
- After **5 years** of service, you receive **1 extra day** per year, and after **10 years**, **2 extra days**.
- Employees who join or leave during the year receive leave pro rata.
- Request leave of **3 or more consecutive days** at least **2 weeks in advance**. Your manager responds within **5
  working days**. Requests can only be refused for a business reason, such as too many team members being away at the
  same time.
- Everyone should take at least **2 consecutive weeks** of leave once a year, to properly recharge.

### Carry-over

You may carry over up to **5 unused days** to the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **30 June**;
after that date they expire, unless you could not take them because of long-term illness or parental leave. Unused
leave is not paid out, except when you leave Corvane.

## 3. Public holidays

Public holidays follow the official calendar of the country of your employment contract. If you work temporarily from
another country, your contract country's holidays still apply. The calendars for the Netherlands, Portugal and Poland
are published on the intranet every December.

## 4. Sick leave

- If you are ill, inform your **manager** by phone or message before **09:30** on your first day of absence, or before
  the start of your shift if it starts earlier, and register the absence in PeoplePortal the same day.
- You do not need a doctor's note from Corvane for short absences, but local law may require one.
- If you are ill for more than **7 calendar days**, the People Team will put you in touch with the **occupational
  health service**, which supports your recovery and return to work.
- Medical details are confidential. You do not have to tell your manager what your illness is, only how long you
  expect to be away and whether there is anything work-related to hand over.
- If you fall ill during annual leave, report it in the same way. The days you were ill are converted back into annual
  leave days.

## 5. Family leave

| Type of leave | Entitlement | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Birth parent leave | 16 weeks | 100% of salary |
| Partner leave (non-birth parent, including adoptive and same-sex parents) | 6 weeks, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption | 100% of salary |
| Adoption and foster leave | 6 weeks per parent | 100% of salary |
| Additional parental leave | As provided by local law, until the child is 8 | As provided by local law |
| Short-term care leave (caring for a sick family member) | Up to 10 days per year | 100% of salary |

Notify the People Team about birth parent leave at least **15 weeks** before the expected due date, so a handover can
be planned. On return from family leave, you can work with reduced hours for 4 weeks while being paid full salary
(the "phased return").

## 6. Other paid leave

| Situation | Paid leave |
|---|---|
| Death of a partner, child or parent | 5 days |
| Death of a sibling, grandparent or parent-in-law | 2 days |
| Your own wedding or civil partnership | 2 days |
| Moving house | 1 day per year |
| Volunteering for a registered charity | 2 days per year |
| Jury service or acting as a witness in court | As long as required |

Bereavement leave can be extended with annual leave or unpaid leave, in agreement with your manager.

## 7. Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

- Unpaid leave of up to **4 weeks** can be agreed with your manager.
- After **5 years** of service, you can request a **sabbatical** of up to **3 months** of unpaid leave. Requests must be
  submitted at least **3 months in advance**. Your job is guaranteed when you return, and pension contributions
  continue during the sabbatical.

## 8. Wellbeing support

- The **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)** is available **24/7**, free and confidential, for employees and their
  household members. It offers up to **6 free counselling sessions per issue per year**, plus legal and financial
  advice.
- Corvane does not receive any information about who uses the EAP or why.

## 9. Recording and responsibilities

- Employees: request and record all leave and absence in PeoplePortal, and set an out-of-office message.
- Managers: approve requests fairly and on time, and make sure their team members actually take their leave.
- People Team: maintain PeoplePortal, answer questions (people@corvane.example) and make sure local law is applied.
