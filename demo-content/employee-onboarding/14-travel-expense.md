# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Principles

Spend Corvane's money as carefully as if it were your own. Business travel should be necessary, safe and
cost-effective, and should consider its environmental impact. You will be reimbursed for reasonable costs you incur
for business purposes; you should not gain or lose money because of business travel.

## 2. Before you travel

- Ask yourself whether the trip is necessary or whether a video call would work.
- Every trip needs **pre-approval** from your manager in **TripDesk**, the Corvane travel booking tool, before
  anything is booked. Trips outside Europe also need approval from your department's VP.
- Book all flights, trains and hotels through TripDesk. Bookings made outside TripDesk are only reimbursed if they
  were at least 10% cheaper and you can show the comparison.
- Book flights at least **14 days in advance** whenever possible.
- Travel insurance is provided by Corvane for all business trips. TripDesk registers your trip automatically, so the
  travel security provider can contact you in an emergency.

## 3. Transport

### Train or plane?

**Rail is the default** for journeys where the train takes **5 hours or less** door to door, for example
Rotterdam–Paris or Rotterdam–Brussels. Flights for these routes need VP approval.

### Flights

| Scheduled flight time | Class |
|---|---|
| Up to 6 hours | Economy class |
| More than 6 hours | Premium economy; business class only with prior VP approval |

First class is never allowed. Choose the lowest logical fare: the cheapest fare that fits your business schedule
without extra stops that add more than 2 hours. Seat selection and one checked bag are reimbursed.

### Cars, taxis and ride-hailing

- Public transport is preferred in cities.
- Taxis and ride-hailing services are allowed when public transport is not practical, late at night (after 22:00), or
  when travelling with heavy equipment.
- Rental cars must be **compact or mid-size**, with full insurance included in the rental. Return the car with a full
  tank, because refuelling fees are not reimbursed.
- Use of a private car for business trips is reimbursed at **€0.23 per kilometre**. Commuting between home and your
  regular office is not a business trip.
- Traffic fines and parking fines are never reimbursed.

## 4. Accommodation

| Location | Maximum per night (room only, incl. taxes) |
|---|---|
| Standard cities | €160 |
| High-cost cities (London, Paris, Zurich, Geneva, Oslo, New York, San Francisco) | €220 |

Book hotels through TripDesk, which shows only hotels within the limits. Staying with friends or family is welcome,
but private accommodation costs (such as a gift for your host) are not reimbursed.

## 5. Meals

- Meals during business travel are reimbursed based on actual receipts, up to **€60 per day** in total.
- **Alcohol** is not reimbursed, except as part of approved client hospitality (see the Gifts, Hospitality and
  Anti-Bribery Policy).
- For a meal with several Corvane employees, the **most senior person** present pays and claims the expense. This
  prevents people from approving their own costs.
- Client meals must list the names of all attendees and their companies, and the business purpose.

## 6. Corvane corporate card

- Employees who travel more than **4 times a year** receive a Corvane corporate card.
- The card is for business expenses only. **Personal use is not allowed**, even if you plan to pay it back.
- Cash withdrawals are allowed only in countries where cards are not widely accepted, up to €200 per trip.
- Report a lost or stolen card to the card provider immediately, and to finance@corvane.example the same day.

## 7. Submitting expense claims

- Submit all expenses in **ExpenseHub** within **30 days** of the end of the trip or of the purchase. Claims submitted
  after **90 days** are not reimbursed.
- Upload a **receipt for every expense above €25**. Photographed receipts are fine; keep the original until the claim
  is paid.
- If you lose a receipt, complete a lost-receipt declaration in ExpenseHub. You may use this at most **twice per
  calendar year**.
- Claims are approved by your manager. **Nobody may approve their own expenses**, or expenses from a trip they
  attended together with the claimant as the most senior person.
- Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## 8. What is not reimbursed

- Personal entertainment, such as minibar, films, gym or spa fees.
- Upgrades to a higher travel class paid without approval.
- Costs for family members or partners travelling with you.
- Fines, penalties and late payment fees on the corporate card.
- Lost or stolen personal items (these may be covered by your own insurance).
- Loyalty-programme memberships. You may keep personal loyalty points earned on business travel, but loyalty
  programmes must never influence your choice of airline or hotel.

## 9. Misuse

Submitting false or inflated expense claims is fraud. It leads to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal,
and Corvane will recover the money. If you make an honest mistake, tell Finance so it can be corrected.
