# Office Safety and Fire Evacuation Procedure

## 1. Emergency numbers

| Situation | Number |
|---|---|
| Fire, medical emergency, police (all Corvane countries) | **112** |
| Corvane internal emergency line (reception and security, 24/7) | **ext. 3333** |
| Security Desk (IT and information security incidents) | ext. 4444 |

When you call 112, call ext. 3333 straight afterwards, so that reception can meet the emergency services at the
entrance and guide them to the right floor.

## 2. Alarm signals

- **Intermittent tone (beep - pause - beep): ALERT.** Stop what you are doing, save your work and prepare to leave.
  Listen for announcements and instructions from fire wardens.
- **Continuous tone: EVACUATE.** Leave the building immediately by the nearest safe exit.

Fire alarms are tested every **Monday at 10:00** with a short continuous tone of up to 10 seconds. A tone at any other
time, or one longer than 10 seconds, is real.

## 3. How to evacuate

1. Leave immediately. **Do not** collect bags, coats or laptops.
2. Close doors behind you if you are the last person in a room, but do not lock them.
3. Use the stairs. **Never use the lifts** during a fire alarm.
4. Follow the green emergency exit signs and the instructions of the fire wardens.
5. Help visitors: hosts are responsible for guiding their visitors out and to the assembly point.
6. Go straight to your office's assembly point and report to your floor's fire warden.
7. Do not re-enter the building until the fire brigade or the head fire warden gives the **all-clear**.

### Assembly points

| Office | Assembly point |
|---|---|
| Rotterdam (HQ) | Assembly Point A: the square in front of the public library, across the street from the main entrance |
| Lisbon | The garden area at the far end of the car park, marked with a green assembly sign |
| Kraków | The pedestrian zone at the north entrance, next to the bicycle racks |

## 4. Fire wardens and first aiders

- **Fire wardens** wear **yellow** high-visibility vests during an evacuation. They sweep their area (including
  toilets and meeting rooms), close doors and report to the head fire warden.
- **First aiders** wear **green** vests. Their names and photos are on the notice board next to every kitchen.
- Every floor must have at least **two** trained fire wardens and **two** trained first aiders. Volunteers receive
  training on paid time and an annual allowance of €150.
- At the assembly point, fire wardens perform a **roll call** using the badge-system attendance list. If you leave the
  office without badging out, tell a fire warden at the assembly point, so nobody searches the building for you.

## 5. Fire drills

A fire drill is held in every office **twice a year**. One of the two drills is unannounced. Everyone in the building
must take part, including visitors and people in meetings with customers. Evacuation of the Rotterdam office should
take no more than **4 minutes**.

## 6. Using a fire extinguisher

Only use an extinguisher if **all** of these are true: you have been trained, the alarm has been raised, the fire is
small (no bigger than a waste-paper bin), and you have a clear escape route behind you. Otherwise, leave.

Remember **PASS**:

- **P**ull the pin.
- **A**im at the base of the fire.
- **S**queeze the handle.
- **S**weep from side to side.

| Extinguisher | Label colour | Use on | Never use on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water | Red | Paper, wood, textiles | Electrical equipment, burning liquids |
| Foam | Cream | Paper, wood, burning liquids | Electrical equipment |
| CO2 | Black | Electrical equipment, server rooms | Deep-fat fires |
| Fire blanket | (red holder in kitchens) | Small cooking-oil fires, clothing fires | Large fires |

## 7. People who need help evacuating

Anyone who may need help to evacuate, permanently or temporarily (for example because of an injury, pregnancy or a
visual or hearing impairment), should ask the People Team for a **Personal Emergency Evacuation Plan (PEEP)**. The
plan assigns two "evacuation buddies" and agrees on the route. Each stairwell has a fire-protected **refuge area**
with an intercom, where a person can wait safely for assistance from the fire brigade.

## 8. First aid and AEDs

First aid kits are located in every kitchen. An **AED** (automated external defibrillator) is located at **reception
on every floor**. In a cardiac emergency: call 112, start chest compressions, and send someone to bring the AED. The
AED gives spoken instructions and can be used by anyone.

## 9. Reporting accidents and near misses

Report every accident, injury, near miss or hazard (such as a trailing cable, a blocked fire exit or a broken chair)
in the **SafetyHub** form on the intranet within **24 hours**. Near-miss reports are just as valuable as accident
reports: they show where an accident is waiting to happen. Serious injuries must also be reported immediately to
ext. 3333.

## 10. Everyday safety rules

- Keep fire exits, corridors and stairwells free of boxes, bags and furniture at all times.
- Never prop open fire doors (doors with a "Fire door - keep shut" sign).
- Personal electrical heaters, kettles and extension-cable chains are not allowed. Electrical equipment brought to the
  office must be safety-tested by Facilities first.
- Report spills in kitchens immediately and use the "wet floor" signs.
