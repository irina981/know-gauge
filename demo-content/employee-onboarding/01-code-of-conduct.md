# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

The Corvane Code of Conduct describes how we work with each other, with customers, with suppliers and with the
communities around our offices in Rotterdam, Lisbon and Kraków. It is the umbrella document for all other Corvane
policies. Where a specific policy (for example the Gifts, Hospitality and Anti-Bribery Policy) gives more detail, the
specific policy applies, but it can never lower the standards set in this Code.

The Code does not try to answer every situation. When the Code is silent, ask yourself the **four questions test**:

1. Is it legal?
2. Is it consistent with this Code and Corvane policies?
3. Would I be comfortable if it were reported in the news or seen by my family?
4. Would I be comfortable explaining it to my manager and to the customer involved?

If the answer to any of these questions is "no" or "I am not sure", stop and ask for advice before you act.

## 2. Our five values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Integrity | We do what we say, we are honest about mistakes, and we never trade ethics for revenue. |
| Respect | We treat everyone with dignity, regardless of role, background or opinion. |
| Ownership | We take responsibility for outcomes, including the problems we find in someone else's area. |
| Customer trust | We protect customer data as if it were our own and we tell customers the truth. |
| Courage | We speak up when something is wrong, even when it is uncomfortable. |

## 3. Who must follow the Code

The Code applies to every employee, contractor, intern and Board member, in every country and on every working day,
including business travel, company events, offsites and online collaboration tools. Managers have additional
responsibilities (see section 9). Third parties that act on Corvane's behalf, such as resellers and agencies, must
accept the Corvane Supplier Code, which is based on this Code.

## 4. Annual certification and training

- New joiners must read the Code and complete the "Code of Conduct Essentials" e-learning within **30 days of their
  start date**.
- Every employee must re-certify the Code once a year. The annual certification window closes on **31 March**.
- Employees who miss the deadline are reported to their manager, and access to the annual bonus review is put on hold
  until certification is complete.

## 5. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when a personal interest could influence, or could appear to influence, your judgement
at work. Having a conflict is not a breach of the Code; **hiding** one is.

Typical conflicts include:

- having a financial interest of more than **5%** in a supplier, customer or competitor;
- hiring, supervising or approving the pay of a close relative or partner;
- working for, or advising, another company in your free time;
- using a Corvane supplier for private work at a discount that is not available to all employees.

You must declare a potential conflict in the Compliance Portal within **30 days** of becoming aware of it, and before
taking any decision that the conflict could affect. Your manager and the Ethics & Compliance Office review the
declaration together and agree on mitigating steps, such as removing you from a supplier selection.

**Outside employment** (paid or unpaid) requires written approval from your manager and the People Team before you
start. Approval is normally given unless the work competes with Corvane, uses Corvane assets or affects your
performance.

## 6. Protecting company assets and information

Corvane assets include laptops, phones, source code, customer data, office equipment, brand and funds. Use them for
legitimate business purposes and protect them from loss, theft and misuse. Limited personal use of IT equipment is
allowed under the Acceptable Use Policy.

Confidential information remains confidential after you leave Corvane. When you leave, you must return all devices
and delete any Corvane information from personal devices on or before your last working day.

**Inside information.** Some Corvane customers are listed companies. If you learn non-public information that could
affect a share price (for example an upcoming acquisition by a customer), you must not trade in those shares or tip
anyone else. This applies even if the trade is small.

## 7. Fair competition and honest business

- We win business on the quality of our products, never through bribes. See the Gifts, Hospitality and Anti-Bribery
  Policy for rules and value limits.
- We never discuss prices, bids, salaries or market allocation with competitors. If a competitor raises these
  topics at a trade association meeting, leave the meeting, ask for your departure to be minuted and inform the Legal
  team the same day.
- Sales and marketing claims must be accurate and supported by evidence. Do not promise features that are not on the
  approved product roadmap.
- Accurate records: all invoices, timesheets and expense claims must be complete and truthful. Creating false records
  is a serious breach, whatever the amount.

## 8. Social media and public communication

Only the Communications team and authorised spokespeople may speak for Corvane in the media. If a journalist contacts
you, do not comment; forward the request to press@corvane.example.

On personal social media you may say that you work at Corvane, but make it clear that the views are your own. Never
share confidential information, customer names (unless the customer is publicly listed as a reference on
corvane.example), internal screenshots or photos of whiteboards.

## 9. Extra responsibilities for managers

Managers must lead by example, make sure their teams complete required training, and create an environment where
people feel safe to raise concerns. A manager who receives a concern must:

1. listen without judgement and thank the person for raising it;
2. never promise an outcome or total secrecy, but explain that information is shared only on a need-to-know basis;
3. pass the concern to the Ethics & Compliance Office within **2 working days**;
4. never try to investigate the concern on their own.

## 10. Speaking up and non-retaliation

If you see or suspect a breach of this Code, speak up. You can talk to your manager, the People Team, the Ethics &
Compliance Office (compliance@corvane.example) or use the independent **Corvane Ethics Line**, which is available
24/7 and allows anonymous reports. The full process is described in the Speak-Up Policy.

Corvane does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or who takes part in an
investigation. Retaliation is itself a breach of this Code and is treated as seriously as the original misconduct.

## 11. Consequences of breaches

Breaches of the Code are handled in a fair and consistent way. Depending on how serious the breach is, consequences
range from coaching and a written warning to dismissal. Breaches that may also be crimes, such as fraud or bribery,
may be reported to the authorities. Knowingly making a false accusation is also a breach of the Code.

## 12. Exceptions and questions

No manager can authorise a breach of this Code. Waivers of any section can only be granted in writing by the Chief
Compliance Officer and, for members of the Executive Team, by the Board. Questions about the Code can be sent to
compliance@corvane.example at any time.
