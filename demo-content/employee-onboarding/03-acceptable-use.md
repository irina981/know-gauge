# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for using Corvane IT resources: laptops, mobile phones, accounts, networks, software,
cloud services and data. It protects Corvane, our customers and you. It works together with the Password and MFA
Standard and the Data Classification and Handling Standard.

## 2. Corvane devices

- Corvane laptops are managed by IT. Full-disk encryption, the endpoint protection agent and automatic updates must
  stay enabled. Never disable or bypass them.
- Install security updates within **3 days** of being prompted. After **7 days**, devices with missing critical
  updates lose access to the corporate network until they are updated.
- Screens lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity. Lock your screen manually (Windows + L, or Control +
  Command + Q on macOS) every time you leave your device, even for a moment.
- Never leave a laptop visible in a parked car. Carry it as hand luggage when travelling.

## 3. Software

- Install software only from the **Corvane Software Center**. If you need a tool that is not available there, raise
  a request with the IT Service Desk (ext. 2222). The Security team reviews new tools within 10 working days.
- Browser extensions follow the same rule: only extensions on the approved list may be installed.
- Pirated or unlicensed software is never allowed.

## 4. Personal use

Limited personal use of Corvane devices and internet access is allowed, provided it:

- does not interfere with your work or the work of others;
- does not involve illegal, offensive, sexually explicit, gambling or hate content;
- does not involve running a private business;
- does not store large personal files (photos, videos, music libraries) on Corvane devices or storage.

Remember that personal use on Corvane systems is not private (see section 9).

## 5. Storing and sharing company data

- Store work files in approved locations only: Corvane OneDrive, team SharePoint sites and approved code repositories.
- **Personal cloud storage** (for example a personal Dropbox or Google Drive) and personal email must never be used for
  Corvane data, not even temporarily.
- Use **Corvane SecureShare** to send Confidential or Restricted files to people outside Corvane.
- **USB storage devices are blocked by default.** If you need one for a business reason, request an encrypted,
  Corvane-issued USB drive from the IT Service Desk. Never plug in a USB device you found or received at an event.

## 6. Personal devices (BYOD)

You may access Corvane email, chat and calendar on a personal phone or tablet only through the Corvane-managed apps
(Outlook and Teams with app protection). The device must have a screen lock with a PIN of at least **6 digits** or
biometrics, and must run an operating system version that still receives security updates. Corvane can remotely wipe
Corvane data (but not your personal data) from the managed apps if the device is lost or you leave the company.

Personal laptops must never be used to access Corvane systems other than through the browser-based web apps.

## 7. Networks and remote access

- Connect to the corporate network only with Corvane devices.
- On public Wi-Fi (hotels, cafés, airports), always connect to the **Corvane VPN** before working. Do not use public
  charging cables or USB charging stations; use your own charger and a power socket.
- Do not set up personal Wi-Fi hotspots, routers or network devices in the office.

## 8. Generative AI tools

- The approved AI assistant is **Corvane Assist**, which runs in Corvane's own cloud tenant. It may be used with
  Public, Internal and Confidential data.
- Public AI tools (consumer chatbots and free online AI services) may only be used with **Public** data.
- **Restricted** data must never be entered into any AI tool, including Corvane Assist.
- You are responsible for checking AI output before you use it. Never paste AI-generated code into a product
  repository without review, and never present AI output to a customer as verified fact without checking it.

## 9. Monitoring

To protect its systems, Corvane logs and monitors the use of its devices, networks, email and cloud services, in line
with applicable law and the Employee Privacy Notice. Monitoring is automated and focused on security threats. Content
is only reviewed by a person when there is a specific security incident or a formal investigation approved by the
CISO and the Data Protection Officer.

## 10. Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen Corvane device, or a personal device with Corvane apps, to the **Security Desk** (ext. 4444 or
security@corvane.example) **within 1 hour** of noticing. Do this before reporting it to the police or the airline.
The Security Desk can lock and wipe the device remotely, but only if it is reported quickly.

## 11. Prohibited activities

The following are never allowed:

- sharing your account or password with anyone, including colleagues and IT staff;
- trying to access systems or data you are not authorised to use, even "just to test";
- running security scanning or hacking tools without written approval from the CISO;
- mining cryptocurrency on Corvane equipment or cloud accounts;
- forwarding Corvane email automatically to an external address;
- using Corvane resources to harass anyone or to send spam or chain letters.

## 12. Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to loss of access and disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal. If you are
not sure whether something is allowed, ask the IT Service Desk before you do it.
