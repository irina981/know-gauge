# Rotterdam Office Guide

The Rotterdam office is Corvane's headquarters on **floors 4 to 6** of the Maaskade Tower, Wilhelminakade 88. It is
open from **07:00 to 20:00** on working days. This guide covers four subjects: fire evacuation, parking and
commuting, the kitchen and coffee, and meeting rooms and visitors.

## 1. Fire evacuation

### 1.1 When the fire alarm sounds

The Rotterdam fire alarm is a **continuous siren** together with **flashing white lights**. When you hear the fire
alarm, stop working and evacuate the building at once. Do not stop to collect bags, coats or laptops, and never
assume a fire alarm is a test unless the fire drill was announced.

### 1.2 Evacuation routes

- During a fire evacuation use the **stairwells**, never the lifts.
- Floors 4 and 5 evacuate through the **north stairwell**; floor 6 evacuates through the **south stairwell**.
- Close doors behind you to slow the spread of fire and smoke.
- Colleagues who cannot use the stairs wait in the **fire-safe refuge area** next to the north stairwell, where a
  fire warden stays with them and an **evacuation chair** is stored.

### 1.3 Assembly point and fire wardens

- The fire assembly point is the **Wilhelminaplein square**, next to the tram stop, about 150 metres from the
  entrance.
- Each floor has two **fire wardens** in **orange vests**. They sweep their floor during the evacuation and report
  to the **head fire warden** at the assembly point.
- Report to your team lead at the assembly point so that nobody is missing. Go back inside only when the head fire
  warden gives the all-clear.

### 1.4 Fire drills and fire equipment

- A **fire drill** is held twice a year, in **March and October**. Fire drills are announced one day in advance.
- **Fire extinguishers** and **fire blankets** hang next to every kitchen and stairwell door.
- If you discover a fire, press the nearest red **fire alarm call point** and call the internal emergency number
  **ext. 3333**. Use a fire extinguisher only on a small fire and only if your way out stays clear.

## 2. Parking and commuting

### 2.1 Car parking

The Maaskade Tower has an underground car park with **40 Corvane parking spaces** on level −2.

- Car parking spaces are **booked in PeoplePortal** up to **14 days** ahead.
- A parking booking that is not used by **10:00** is released for other drivers.
- Parking overnight or at weekends is not allowed without the office manager's permission.
- **Motorcycles** park in the marked bay next to the car park ramp; no booking is needed.

### 2.2 Electric car charging

- **Eight parking spaces have electric car chargers.** Book a charging space separately in PeoplePortal.
- Move your car as soon as it is charged, and after **4 hours** of charging at the latest, so others can charge.
- Charging is free for employees; the charging cable stays connected to the charger.

### 2.3 Cycling to work

- The **bicycle storage** on level −1 has room for **120 bikes** and opens with your badge.
- Showers, lockers and a bicycle pump are next to the bicycle storage.
- Corvane pays a **cycling allowance of €0.23 per kilometre** for every day you cycle to the office.

### 2.4 Public transport

- Corvane reimburses **public transport** costs in full. Submit your monthly travel card statement in PeoplePortal.
- The nearest tram stop is **Wilhelminaplein** (tram lines 20, 23 and 25). Rotterdam Centraal train station is
  10 minutes away by tram.
- Colleagues who share a car can register as a **carpool**; carpools get priority on parking bookings.

## 3. Kitchen and coffee

### 3.1 Coffee and drinks

- Each floor has a kitchen. The **barista corner** on floor 5 is the main coffee point.
- Coffee, tea, milk and plant-based milk are **free** for everyone.
- The barista makes coffee from **08:30 to 11:30**; at other times use the bean-to-cup coffee machines.
- If a coffee machine shows a cleaning or descaling message, report it to the office manager; do not open the
  coffee machine yourself.

### 3.2 Fruit and snacks

- Fresh fruit is delivered to every kitchen on **Monday and Thursday** mornings.
- Snacks in the kitchen cupboards are free; take a fair share.

### 3.3 Fridge and food rules

- Label your food in the kitchen fridge with your **name and the date**.
- Kitchen fridges are **emptied every Friday at 16:00**; food left in the fridge is thrown away.
- Keep food with **nuts** on the separate shelf marked for allergens.

### 3.4 Keeping the kitchen clean

- Put used cups and plates in the **dishwasher**, not in the kitchen sink.
- The last person in the kitchen in the evening starts the dishwasher.
- Wipe the kitchen counter after use.

### 3.5 Team lunch

- **Team lunch** is served in the floor 6 canteen every **Wednesday at 12:30**.
- Register for team lunch in PeoplePortal by **Tuesday noon**, and mention any dietary requirements.

## 4. Meeting rooms and visitors

### 4.1 Booking meeting rooms

Rotterdam has **14 meeting rooms**, named after Dutch rivers (Maas, Rijn, Waal, IJssel and so on).

- Book meeting rooms in your **calendar** by adding the room to the meeting invitation.
- **Check in on the room panel** when the meeting starts. A meeting room booking is released automatically if
  nobody checks in within **10 minutes**.
- Meeting rooms can be booked for at most **4 hours** at a time; longer bookings need the office manager's
  approval.

### 4.2 Large meeting rooms and video meetings

- The **Maas room** on floor 6 holds **20 people** and has the **video wall** used for all-hands meetings.
- Every other meeting room has a screen and a conference camera for video meetings.
- Leave the meeting room tidy: wipe the whiteboard and take your cups.

### 4.3 Registering visitors

- Register visitors in PeoplePortal at least **one working day** before the visit.
- Visitors sign the visitor log at **reception on floor 4** and receive a **yellow visitor badge**.
- The host collects the visitor at reception and accompanies the visitor at all times.

### 4.4 When visitors leave

- Visitors return their visitor badge at reception when they leave.
- Visitor Wi-Fi details are given at reception together with the visitor badge.
