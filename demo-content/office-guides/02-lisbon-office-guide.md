# Lisbon Office Guide

The Lisbon office is on the **ground floor and floor 1** of the Casa do Rio building, Rua da Ribeira 12, near the
river Tagus. It is open from **08:00 to 19:00** on working days. This guide covers four subjects: fire evacuation,
parking and commuting, the kitchen and coffee, and meeting rooms and visitors.

## 1. Fire evacuation

### 1.1 When the fire alarm sounds

The Lisbon fire alarm is an **intermittent bell** combined with a recorded evacuation message in Portuguese and
English. When you hear the fire alarm, stop working and evacuate at once, without collecting bags or coats.

### 1.2 Evacuation routes

- From floor 1, evacuate by the **outside staircase** at the back of the building.
- From the ground floor, evacuate by the **main entrance** or the **garden door**, whichever is nearer.
- Never use the lift during a fire alarm.
- Colleagues who cannot use the outside staircase wait at the **fire-safe landing** on floor 1, where a fire warden
  stays with them until the fire brigade arrives.

### 1.3 Assembly point and fire wardens

- The fire assembly point is the **riverside promenade**, opposite the building, at the green **assembly point
  sign**.
- Lisbon has **four fire wardens**, wearing **yellow vests**. The **office manager** acts as the head fire warden.
- Fire wardens check the meeting rooms, the kitchen and the toilets during the evacuation.
- Stay at the assembly point until the head fire warden gives the all-clear, even if the fire alarm stops.

### 1.4 Fire drills and fire equipment

- **Fire drills** are held twice a year, in **April and November**.
- **Fire extinguishers** hang next to both exits and in the kitchen; a **fire blanket** hangs in the kitchen.
- If you discover a fire, use the nearest **fire alarm call point**, call the internal emergency number
  **ext. 3333**, and call the fire brigade on **112**.

## 2. Parking and commuting

### 2.1 Car parking

The Casa do Rio building has no car park of its own.

- Corvane rents **12 parking spaces** in the public **Ribeira car park**, 300 metres away.
- Parking spaces are shared and **booked in PeoplePortal** the day before, before **18:00**.
- Show the Corvane **parking card** at the car park barrier; the parking card is collected from the office manager.

### 2.2 Electric car charging

- There are **no electric car chargers** at the Ribeira car park.
- The nearest public electric car chargers are at the **Cais do Sodré** station car park; charging costs there are
  reimbursed through PeoplePortal.

### 2.3 Cycling and scooters

- Bicycles and **electric scooters** are stored in the locked **courtyard shed**, which holds **30 bikes**.
- Charge electric scooters only at the marked socket in the courtyard shed.
- Corvane does not pay a cycling allowance in Lisbon; the public transport pass is offered instead.

### 2.4 Public transport

- Corvane pays a **monthly Navegante public transport pass** for every Lisbon employee.
- Request the public transport pass in PeoplePortal before the **20th of the month** for the next month.
- The office is a **5-minute walk** from Cais do Sodré, served by the metro, trains to Cascais and the ferries
  across the river.

## 3. Kitchen and coffee

### 3.1 Coffee and drinks

- Lisbon has one large kitchen, the **Cozinha**, on the ground floor.
- Coffee is made with the **espresso machine**; coffee pods are free.
- Tea, milk and plant-based milk are free as well.
- Empty the espresso machine's pod container when it is full.

### 3.2 Pastries and fruit

- Fresh **pastries** (pastéis de nata) are delivered every **Friday morning** for the whole office.
- A fruit bowl in the kitchen is refilled every Monday.

### 3.3 Fridge and food rules

- Label your food in the kitchen fridge with your **name and the date**.
- The kitchen fridge is **emptied every Thursday at 17:00**.
- Keep food with **nuts** on the allergen shelf at the bottom of the fridge.

### 3.4 Keeping the kitchen clean

- Wash your own cups and put them back on the kitchen shelf.
- The kitchen dishwasher is run by the cleaning team at the end of the day.
- Wipe the kitchen table after lunch.

### 3.5 Team lunch

- **Team lunch** is on **Tuesdays at 13:00** on the terrace when the weather allows, otherwise in the kitchen.
- Register for team lunch in PeoplePortal by **Monday noon**, with any dietary requirements.

## 4. Meeting rooms and visitors

### 4.1 Booking meeting rooms

Lisbon has **six meeting rooms**, named after Portuguese explorers (Magellan, Dias, Gama, Cabral, Corte-Real and
Caboto).

- Book meeting rooms in your **calendar** by adding the room to the meeting invitation.
- **Check in on the room panel** when the meeting starts. If nobody checks in within **15 minutes**, the meeting
  room booking is released.
- Meeting rooms can be booked for at most **3 hours** at a time.

### 4.2 Large meeting rooms and video meetings

- The **Magellan room** on floor 1 holds **12 people** and is the only meeting room with a **video wall**.
- The other meeting rooms have a screen and a speakerphone for video meetings.
- Leave the meeting room tidy and switch off the screen.

### 4.3 Registering visitors

- Register visitors in PeoplePortal at least **two hours** before the visit.
- Visitors are welcomed at the **ground-floor reception** and receive a **blue visitor badge**.
- Visitors stay with their host at all times.

### 4.4 Visitor Wi-Fi and leaving

- Visitors may use the guest Wi-Fi network **Corvane-Guest**; the password is printed on the back of the visitor
  badge.
- Visitors return the visitor badge at reception when they leave.
