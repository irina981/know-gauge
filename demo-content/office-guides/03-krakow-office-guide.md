# Kraków Office Guide

The Kraków office is on **floor 3** of the Wawel Business Park, building B, ulica Przemysłowa 7. It is open from
**07:30 to 19:30** on working days. This guide covers four subjects: fire evacuation, parking and commuting, the
kitchen and coffee, and meeting rooms and visitors.

## 1. Fire evacuation

### 1.1 When the fire alarm sounds

The Kraków fire alarm is a **two-tone siren**. A recorded evacuation message tells you which stairwell to use.
When you hear the fire alarm, stop working and evacuate at once, without collecting bags or coats.

### 1.2 Evacuation routes

- Evacuate by the **east stairwell**. If the east stairwell is blocked, use the **west stairwell**.
- Never use the lifts during a fire alarm.
- Colleagues who cannot use the stairs wait in the **fire-safe lobby** of the east stairwell, where a fire warden
  stays with them and an **evacuation chair** is available.

### 1.3 Assembly point and fire wardens

- The fire assembly point is the **car park in front of building B**, at **row A**, marked with a green assembly
  point sign.
- Kraków has **three fire wardens** in **green vests**. Each fire warden sweeps one zone of floor 3 during the
  evacuation.
- The fire wardens report to the **business park's security officer**, who acts as the head fire warden for the
  whole building.
- Wait at the assembly point until the security officer gives the all-clear.

### 1.4 Fire drills and fire equipment

- **Fire drills** are organised by the business park **once a year, in May**. Corvane joins every fire drill.
- **Fire extinguishers** and **fire blankets** are next to both stairwell doors and in the kitchen.
- If you discover a fire, use the nearest **fire alarm call point** and call the internal emergency number
  **ext. 3333**; the business park's security desk can be reached on **ext. 5100**.

## 2. Parking and commuting

### 2.1 Car parking

The Wawel Business Park has a large outdoor car park.

- Corvane has **25 reserved parking spaces** in **row C**.
- Parking spaces do not need to be booked: park on any free Corvane space in row C.
- Display the **Corvane parking permit** behind the windscreen; request the parking permit from the office manager.

### 2.2 Electric car charging

- **Four parking spaces** in row C have **electric car chargers**.
- Electric car chargers are booked in PeoplePortal for up to **3 hours** of charging.
- Move your car to a normal parking space when charging is finished.

### 2.3 Cycling to work

- The covered **bicycle shelter** next to building B holds **40 bikes**.
- Lock your bicycle to the bicycle stands; the bicycle shelter is not locked at night.
- Corvane pays a **cycling allowance of PLN 1 per kilometre** for every day you cycle to the office.

### 2.4 Public transport

- Corvane reimburses **50% of a monthly tram and bus ticket** (MPK Kraków).
- Upload the public transport ticket in PeoplePortal by the **5th of the following month**.
- Bus lines **128 and 184** stop in front of the business park; the city centre is 20 minutes away by bus.

## 3. Kitchen and coffee

### 3.1 Coffee and drinks

- The Kraków kitchen is called **Kuchnia** and is next to the open-plan area.
- Coffee comes from two **bean-to-cup coffee machines**; coffee is free.
- Tea, milk and plant-based milk are free as well.
- Refill the coffee machine's water tank when it is empty.

### 3.2 Fruit and snacks

- A **fruit basket** is delivered to the kitchen every **Wednesday** morning.
- Biscuits and snacks in the kitchen cupboard are free.

### 3.3 Fridge and food rules

- Label your food in the kitchen fridge with your **name and the date**.
- The kitchen fridge is **emptied every Friday at 15:00**.
- Keep food with **nuts** in the box marked for allergens.

### 3.4 Keeping the kitchen clean

- Load your cups and plates into the **dishwasher**.
- Whoever empties a clean dishwasher earns a **"kitchen hero"** mention in the weekly office update.
- Wipe the kitchen counter after use.

### 3.5 Team lunch

- **Pierogi lunch** is ordered for everyone on the **last Friday of the month** and eaten in the kitchen.
- Register for the pierogi lunch in PeoplePortal by **Wednesday**, with any dietary requirements.

## 4. Meeting rooms and visitors

### 4.1 Booking meeting rooms

Kraków has **eight meeting rooms**, named after Polish mountains (Rysy, Giewont, Babia Góra, Śnieżka and so on).

- Book meeting rooms in your **calendar** by adding the room to the meeting invitation.
- **Check in on the room panel** when the meeting starts. A meeting room booking is released if nobody checks in
  within **10 minutes**.
- Meeting rooms can be booked for at most **4 hours** at a time.

### 4.2 Large meeting rooms and video meetings

- The **Rysy room** holds **16 people** and has the **video wall**.
- The other meeting rooms have a screen and a conference camera for video meetings.
- Leave the meeting room tidy and return borrowed adapters to the meeting room drawer.

### 4.3 Registering visitors

- Register visitors in PeoplePortal at least **one working day** before the visit, because the business park's
  security desk needs their names.
- Visitors collect a **white visitor badge** at the **building B security desk** and show an identity document.
- The host meets the visitor at the security desk and accompanies the visitor at all times.

### 4.4 When visitors leave

- Visitors return the visitor badge to the security desk when they leave.
- Visitor Wi-Fi details are printed on the visitor badge.
