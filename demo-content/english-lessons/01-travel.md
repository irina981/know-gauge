# Lesson 1 – Travel and Holidays

This lesson is about travelling and holidays. It has four parts: vocabulary, grammar (present perfect and past
simple), common mistakes, and useful phrases.

## 1. Vocabulary: travel and holidays

### 1.1 Journeys

- A **journey** is the act of travelling from one place to another, especially over a long distance: *The journey
  from London to Edinburgh takes four hours by train.*
- A **trip** is a journey to a place and back, usually short and for a particular purpose: *a business trip, a
  day trip to the coast.*
- **Travel** is usually an uncountable noun for travelling in general: *Air travel is getting more expensive.*
- A **voyage** is a long journey by sea or in space.

### 1.2 At the airport

- You **check in** at the desk, where you **drop off** your luggage and get a **boarding pass**.
- **Hand luggage** (or carry-on luggage) is the bag you take onto the plane.
- At the **gate** you **board** the plane; a plane **takes off** and **lands**.
- A **delay** means the flight leaves later than planned; a **cancellation** means it does not leave at all.

### 1.3 Accommodation

- A **hostel** is cheap accommodation, often with shared rooms; a **bed and breakfast (B&B)** is a small guest house
  that serves breakfast.
- When you **book** a room, you make a **reservation**; you **check in** on arrival and **check out** when you leave.
- **Full board** includes all meals, **half board** includes breakfast and dinner.

## 2. Grammar: present perfect and past simple

### 2.1 Present perfect

- Form: **have/has + past participle**: *I have visited Rome. She has never flown.*
- Use it for experiences in your life **up to now**, when the exact time is not important: *Have you ever been to
  Japan?*
- Use it for actions that started in the past and **continue now**, with **for** and **since**: *We have lived here
  for ten years / since 2016.*
- Use it for recent actions with a result now, often with **just**, **already** and **yet**: *I've just booked the
  tickets. Have you packed yet?*

### 2.2 Past simple

- Form: verb + **-ed** for regular verbs (*visited, travelled*); irregular verbs have their own form (*went, flew,
  saw*).
- Use it for **finished actions at a specific time in the past**: *I visited Rome in 2019. We flew to Lisbon last
  week.*
- Time expressions with the past simple: **yesterday, last year, in 2019, two days ago, when I was a child**.

### 2.3 Choosing between them

- *I have been to Paris* (an experience, no time given) – *I went to Paris last summer* (a finished time).
- Questions with **When…?** use the past simple: *When did you arrive?* (not *When have you arrived?*)
- A conversation often starts with the present perfect and continues with the past simple: *Have you ever been to
  Greece? – Yes, I went there in 2021. It was wonderful.*

## 3. Common mistakes: travel

### 3.1 Words that are often confused

- ✗ *I had a nice travel.* → ✓ *I had a nice trip.* (**travel** is usually uncountable)
- ✗ *How was your journey to Madrid for the weekend?* when you mean the whole holiday → ✓ *How was your trip?*
- ✗ *I lost the flight.* → ✓ *I missed the flight.*

### 3.2 Grammar mistakes

- ✗ *I have been to Spain last year.* → ✓ *I went to Spain last year.* (a finished time needs the past simple)
- ✗ *I am here since Monday.* → ✓ *I have been here since Monday.*
- ✗ *I live in this city for five years.* → ✓ *I have lived in this city for five years.*

### 3.3 Prepositions

- ✗ *We arrived to the airport.* → ✓ *We arrived at the airport.* (**arrive at** a building, **arrive in** a city or
  country)
- ✗ *I go on holidays in August.* → ✓ *I go on holiday in August.* (British English)

## 4. Useful phrases: travel

### 4.1 At the hotel

- *I have a reservation under the name Petrović.*
- *Could I have a room with a view of the sea, please?*
- *What time is check-out?*
- *Is breakfast included in the price?*

### 4.2 Asking for directions

- *Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to the station?*
- *Is it within walking distance?*
- *Go straight on, take the second left, and it's on your right.*

### 4.3 Problems while travelling

- *My flight has been cancelled. Could you put me on the next one?*
- *My luggage hasn't arrived. Where can I report it?*
- *I'd like to make a complaint about the room.*
