# Lesson 4 – Technology and the Internet

This lesson is about computers, phones and the internet. It has four parts: vocabulary, grammar (the passive voice),
common mistakes, and useful phrases.

## 1. Vocabulary: technology and the internet

### 1.1 Devices

- A **device** is a machine or tool, such as a **laptop**, a **tablet** or a **smartphone**.
- You **charge** a phone with a **charger**; when the battery is empty, the phone is **flat** (British English) or
  **dead**.
- **Hardware** is the physical parts of a computer; **software** is the programs.

### 1.2 Using the internet

- You **go online** or **browse** the internet with a **browser**.
- You **download** files from the internet and **upload** files to it.
- You **log in** (or **sign in**) with a **username** and a **password**, and **log out** when you finish.
- A **link** takes you to another page; you **click on** it.

### 1.3 Social media and messages

- You **post** a photo, **share** a link, and **follow** people on social media.
- A post that spreads very quickly online **goes viral**.
- You **text** someone (send a text message) or **send a voice message**.

## 2. Grammar: the passive voice

### 2.1 Form

- Form: **be + past participle**: *English is spoken all over the world.*
- Present simple passive: *The data is stored in the cloud.*
- Past simple passive: *The first iPhone was released in 2007.*
- Present perfect passive: *Your password has been changed.*

### 2.2 When to use the passive

- Use the passive when the **action** is more important than **who** does it: *The website is updated every day.*
- Use it when the person who does the action is **unknown** or **obvious**: *My laptop was stolen.*
- The passive is common in **technical** and **formal** writing and in news reports.

### 2.3 The agent with by

- Add **by** + the person or thing that does the action when it is important: *The World Wide Web was invented by
  Tim Berners-Lee.*
- Leave out the agent when it adds no information: *The update was installed* (not *…was installed by someone*).
- Modal verbs in the passive: **modal + be + past participle**: *Passwords must be changed every year.*

## 3. Common mistakes: technology

### 3.1 Words that are often confused

- ✗ *I need to charge my mobile.* is fine, but ✗ *I need to load my phone.* → ✓ *I need to charge my phone.*
- ✗ *Can you send me the informations?* → ✓ *Can you send me the information?* (**information** is uncountable)
- ✗ *I lost my password.* (when you mean you can't remember it) → ✓ *I forgot my password.*

### 3.2 Grammar mistakes

- ✗ *The file was send yesterday.* → ✓ *The file was sent yesterday.* (use the past participle)
- ✗ *The app is develop by a small team.* → ✓ *The app is developed by a small team.*
- ✗ *My account has hacked.* → ✓ *My account has been hacked.*

### 3.3 Prepositions

- ✗ *I saw it in the internet.* → ✓ *I saw it on the internet.*
- ✗ *Click to the link.* → ✓ *Click on the link.*

## 4. Useful phrases: technology

### 4.1 Asking for technical help

- *My laptop keeps freezing. Do you know what's wrong with it?*
- *Have you tried turning it off and on again?*
- *I can't connect to the Wi-Fi. What's the password?*

### 4.2 Online meetings

- *Can everyone hear me?*
- *You're on mute.*
- *I'll share my screen.*
- *Sorry, you broke up for a moment. Could you repeat that?*

### 4.3 Talking about technology

- *I'm not very good with technology.*
- *I spend too much time on my phone.*
- *I've just downloaded a really useful app.*
